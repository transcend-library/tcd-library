{%- comment -%} Relative timestamp, e.g. "3 years ago". Usage: {% include relative-time.html date="2023-04-07" %} or: {% include relative-time.html date=page.updated prefix="on " %} Renders the exact date; assets/js/relative-time.js swaps in the relative text in the browser (so it never goes stale) and keeps the exact date as a tooltip. Without JS, `prefix` + the exact date is shown (e.g. "on 17 November 2022"). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _exact = include.date | date: "%-d %B %Y" -%} {{ include.prefix }}{{ _exact }}
