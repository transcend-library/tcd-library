# Disclaimer

The Transcend Library is written by and for members of our community. It brings together what we've learned from our own experiences, and from the people who have helped us along the way, so that you don't have to work everything out alone.

Please read the guides with the following in mind:

## This is not medical, legal or professional advice

Nothing on this site is a substitute for advice from a qualified doctor, psychiatrist, psychologist, lawyer or other professional who knows your situation. Hormone therapy and surgery have real, sometimes permanent, effects on your body. Talk to a healthcare professional before starting, changing or stopping any treatment.

## Information can go out of date

Rules, routes, prices, wait times and the people involved change, sometimes quickly. Each guide shows when it was published and last updated; parts of it may still reflect how things were at the time it was written. If something you read here doesn't match what you're told, trust the current, official source, and let us know so we can fix it.

## Experiences differ

Much of what's here comes from personal experience. What happened to one person at a clinic, a hospital or an office may not happen to you.

## Listings are not endorsements

Doctors, clinics, counsellors and other services are listed because members of our community have found them useful or relevant, not because we've vetted them, and not as a recommendation. Check with the provider directly before you go.

The library does not endorse self-managing or DIY-ing hormone replacement therapy. Where we write about it, it's to help people who are already doing so stay safer.

## External links

We link to other websites for convenience. We don't control them and aren't responsible for their content, accuracy or availability.

## Keep it within the community

Each guide carries a note about who it's meant for. Please respect it, and don't circulate the guides publicly.

## If you need help now

If you're in crisis or thinking about suicide or self-harm, please reach out to one of the [helplines on our friends page]({{ '/friends/' | relative_url }}#helplines-title).

## Corrections

Spotted something wrong or out of date? Suggest edits in #library-suggestions, or reach out to a librarian on Transcend.
