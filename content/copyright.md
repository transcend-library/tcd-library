## Animated home page cover

The home page cover combines and adapts three CodePens, each used under the MIT licence:

- [Morning Sun Gradient (no div)](https://codepen.io/niallains/pen/RwjNPjM) by Niall (@niallains): the sky gradient. Its moon and skyline have been replaced with custom artwork.
- [Night Sky with twinkling stars](https://codepen.io/getdesigning/pen/xbGjJX) by @getdesigning: the rotating star field and twinkling stars.
- [Clouds over Springfield](https://codepen.io/z4p0/pen/ExmjMJx) by @z4p0: the drifting daytime clouds, animated here with CSS instead of GSAP.
