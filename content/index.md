# Welcome to the Transcend Library!

We established this resource space {% include relative-time.html date="2022-11-17" prefix="on " %} largely because of a central need that many of us will experience — navigating gender-affirming care in this city-state.

The fact is that trans healthcare has changed, yet again, with Circular 44/2026, and that the pathways to care continue to be convoluted and tied up and nearly impossible for many, especially those under 21.

And as anti-trans rhetoric rises, it's hard to actually know who to trust. We are working to update these guides — while much of its contents are mostly from 2023 and last updated in 2024, many of the things within continue to be applicable, and we hope they continue to be of good use.
