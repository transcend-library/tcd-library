This is a list of private doctors and general practitioners (GPs) in Singapore that the community has used for gender-affirming care, mostly for blood tests and, at some clinics, hormone therapy (HRT).

It can help if you:

- need blood tests to keep track of your hormone levels, especially if you’re self-managing HRT;
- want a private option while you wait for a referral through the public healthcare system; or
- aren’t eligible for subsidised public healthcare.

A few things to keep in mind:

- **This list is incomplete, and some of it is based on older information.** Call the clinic before you go to check what they offer and how much it costs.
- **Private care costs more.** GPs are generally cheaper than specialists like endocrinologists. For ongoing HRT, the public healthcare route is usually the more affordable and reliable option. See the [guide to gender-affirming healthcare]({{site.baseurl}}/guide/gender-affirming-healthcare/).
- **If you’re under 21,** MOH’s 2026 guidelines apply to private doctors too, so most will not be able to prescribe HRT to you. See [gender-affirming care for minors]({{site.baseurl}}/guide/gender-affirming-healthcare/below-21/).
