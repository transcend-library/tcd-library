Singapore’s healthcare system is organised into three clusters - the National University Health System, the National Healthcare Group and SingHealth.

The National University Health System is home to [NUH and its gender clinic]({{site.baseurl}}/guide/gender-affirming-healthcare/#h-0d3633fb-7d1f-45ca-8400-89c2b680ac03), while SingHealth is home to [CGH and its gender clinic]({{site.baseurl}}/guide/gender-affirming-healthcare/#h-89e707a0-1b2b-47a0-8204-0a211323231c). The graphic below has more on the specific hospitals and polyclinics that fall under each cluster.

Some services may be “region-locked” while others are not. As trans people do see specialists to acquire gender-affirming care, cross-cluster referrals often happen - like from [IMH Psychology to CGH/NUH Endocrinology]({{site.baseurl}}/guide/gender-affirming-healthcare/#h-0dd2d765-fa85-4594-a93d-fe8b756f174c), for instance. You may be asked to go to another hospital to fill your prescription as well.

*How do the clusters affect us?*

As far as we know - while all three clusters use the National Electronic Health Record, your gender and how it is recorded, as well as your salutations recorded in the system differ across all three healthcare clusters.

This means that it is entirely possible for a person to have their gender recorded correctly in one database even if their gender is recorded differently \[as their legal gender, for instance\].

The healthcare system is notoriously finicky when it comes to determining your gender - if you present as the gender you identify with when they see you in the first instance, it is likely that they can and will mark you as that gender even if it differs from your legal gender. There have also been some instances where there has been a change in the recorded gender after one has presented as the gender they identify with.

In the case of NUH, it seems that referrals from outside their cluster to the Gender Care Clinic may be region-locked and be specific to people with addresses in the west. This may present itself as a tricky problem, especially if you wish to go to a particular clinic to ask for documents to be signed (like a [medical examination report]({{site.baseurl}}/guide/social-transition/#h-e4741761-ece0-4d5d-a5b8-98e1f08e0ae2) for a change in gender marker).
